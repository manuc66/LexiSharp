---
title: Support ticket triage notes
category: support
tags: [support, triage, escal]
---
Triage sorts tickets by blast radius before it sorts them by severity. A ticket
that affects every account outranks a ticket that affects one loud customer, even
when the second one is angrier. The category field records who owns the ticket
after triage, not what the ticket is about, so a ticket about the login page can
carry the category of the billing team when the outage is upstream of billing.
