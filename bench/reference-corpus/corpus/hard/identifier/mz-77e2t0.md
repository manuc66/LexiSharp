title: Drive MZ-77E2T0 assembly variant
category: hardware
tags: [hardware, assembly, recall]
---
The MZ-77E2T0 assembly was superseded in production. A teardown of unit
MZ-77E2T0 serial 4471 found a cracked solder joint on the power regulator and a
misaligned heatsink. Field service replaced the unit under warranty.
